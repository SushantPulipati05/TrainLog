Progress tab — "Needs attention" + sorting

"Sets this week" is gone. In its place: a short Needs attention card (exercises that have stalled or quietly dropped out), and a way to sort Your exercises. Only the app's colours, no emoji — the icons are drawn in the app's orange and grey. Made-up data.

AOne list + sort chips

Needs attention is a single list of up to 3 rows; the icon says which kind (flat line = stalled, clock = not done lately). Sorting is a row of 3 chips right above the list — always visible, one tap.

BGrouped + sort menu

Needs attention split into two small labelled groups, Stalled and Not done lately. Sorting is a small "Most used ▾" button that opens a sheet — less clutter, one extra tap.

B· sort menu open

What tapping "Most used ▾" shows. Each option says what it does in one line.

How it decides — Stalled: an exercise you've done at least 6 times whose top set hasn't beaten its earlier best in the last 4 sessions. Not done lately: one you did regularly (4+ sessions) but not in the last 3 weeks. At most 3 rows show, stalled first; tap a row to open the exercise. If nothing qualifies, the card isn't shown at all.

Sorts — Most used: most sessions first (default). Recently done: latest session first. Biggest gain: largest top-set increase over 3 months first.
