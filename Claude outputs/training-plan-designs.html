Training plan + gym reminder

Two new onboarding steps (they slot in after "weekly target", so the progress bar goes to 9), a Training plan screen reached from Profile, and the notification itself. The split days get laid over your training days in order, so every day of the week has a fixed workout you can tap to change. Tell me what to change, or "build it".
