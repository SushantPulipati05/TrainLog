2Nothing to say + "Biggest gain"

No notes, so the card isn't there. Sorted by Biggest gain: change in estimated 1RM over 3 months, so more reps at the same weight counts too.

Weight PR — heavier than any earlier session of that exercise. Rep PR — more reps than ever at a weight you've already lifted ("60 kg × 9, best at 60 kg was × 7").
Form check-in — only when, in the last 5 sessions of one exercise: 3+ weight PRs, each jump 7.5% or more, and reps falling as weight rises; never in the first 8 weeks of that exercise.
Not done lately — done 4+ times, but not in the last 3 weeks. Small win — more reps at the same weight, or a higher estimated 1RM, than a month ago.
At most 3 notes, form check-ins first. Biggest gain uses estimated 1RM (weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30)), shown in brackets so it's clear what's compared.
